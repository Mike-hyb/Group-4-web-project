How we protect your account

Sign-in and session

Passwords are hashed with a unique salt and never stored in readable form. Every sign-in needs a one-time code. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts. Sessions expire after 15 minutes, or after 5 minutes without activity.

Your data

Personal details and loan information are encrypted at rest with AES-256. All traffic uses HTTPS. Staff can only see what their role requires.

Staying safe from phishing

We will never ask for your password or one-time code by email, text or phone. Check the last sign-in time on your dashboard; if it is not you, change your password.
